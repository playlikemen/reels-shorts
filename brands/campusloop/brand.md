# CampusLoop

- Site: https://campusloop.space/ · Instagram: @campusloop.space · Bio link: https://campusloop.space/ig
- Tagline: "Your campus. Only your campus."
- Audience: students at Indian colleges, 17 to 23. Launch campus: BIT Mesra.
- Voice: student to student, casual, a little Hinglish is fine. Never corporate, never an ad.
- Colours: violet `#6A3DE8`, reward yellow `#FFD23F`, crush pink `#FF4D8D`.
- Call to action: "Find your college at campusloop.space" or "Send this to the friend who ...".
- Posting: one reel a day at 19:30 IST. `max_reels_per_day` in `brand.json` enforces it.

## What the numbers say (insights pulled 2026-11-19, 36 reels)

| Format | Views | Shares | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder on camera, hostel room | 7,492 | 34 (22 comments) | Best format. Do three a week. |
| Relatable campus truth (75% attendance) | 2,033 | 103 | Best automated format. |
| Fantasy, horror and riddle stories | 200 to 1,400 | 0 to 3 | Stop. |
| Product ads | about 200, 2 to 4 s watched | 0 | Stop. |

Thirteen reels went out on 11 November and most stalled under 500 views. Never post more than one a day.

## Facts that may be used (check the site before use)

- College email sign-up keeps each student in their own campus. A personal email gets Campus Preview.
- Anonymous posts never show the author's name.
- Three reports hide a post until a person reviews it, within 24 hours.
- Notes, past papers and courses are free. Read the live counts from the home page.
- An invite gives 50 Loop Points when the friend verifies a college email.

## Never do

- Never present a written-for-the-reel confession, poll or result as real, and never name a real student.
- Never invent user counts or testimonials.
- Campus Match content stays clean and says 18+.
- See `playbook.md` for the weekly plan.
