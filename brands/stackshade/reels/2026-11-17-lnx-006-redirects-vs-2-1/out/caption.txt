Ever wondered what `2>&1` means in Linux? 🤔

Every Linux process has 3 standard streams:
0️⃣ stdin (Standard Input)
1️⃣ stdout (Standard Output)
2️⃣ stderr (Standard Error)

`2>&1` redirects stream 2 into stream 1, ensuring all error messages land in your log file!
