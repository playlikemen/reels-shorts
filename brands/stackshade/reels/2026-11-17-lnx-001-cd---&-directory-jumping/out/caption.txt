Stop typing 'cd ../../..' in your terminal! 🛑

Here's the single fastest Linux navigation trick you need to know:

✨ `cd -` takes you back to your previous working directory instantly.
✨ `pushd <dir>` and `popd` lets you navigate deep paths like a stack.

Save this for your next terminal session! 🚀
