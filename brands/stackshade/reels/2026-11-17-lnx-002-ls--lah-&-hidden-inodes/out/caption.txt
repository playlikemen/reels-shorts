Stop typing plain 'ls' in your terminal! ❌

Run `ls -lah` instead to get:
1. Detailed permissions & ownership
2. Hidden dotfiles (`.env`, `.bashrc`)
3. Human-readable file sizes (MB/GB instead of raw bytes)

Add `-i` to see the internal inode number! 🐧
