# StackShade

Audience: students and working developers who want to understand how real systems work.
Promise: one engineering idea, explained visually, in under a minute.

Topics: system design, databases, backend, DevOps, developer tools, AI engineering, language and runtime internals.
Voice: direct, concrete, a little dry. "Here is what actually happens." No hype words, no "game changer".
Good hooks: a common belief that is wrong ("UPDATE does not overwrite"), a number ("pay a tenth"), a hidden mechanism ("Git is a key-value store").
Avoid: news without a primary source, opinions on which tool is best, anything that needs a company logo.

Look: `brutal` in the engine's default violet, mint and pink for numbers and comparisons; `clean` with the orange accent for how-it-works diagrams.
Voice: en-US-AndrewNeural at +8%. CTA: "Follow StackShade for more."
Slot: 19:00 IST daily. Instagram and YouTube Shorts.
